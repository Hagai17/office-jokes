# Candidates — Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes

Fourteen office jokes were drafted and duelled pairwise on the judge's own
criteria: a real joke (setup plus turn), an unexpected turn, a line that rings
true about office life, tight wording, and a fresh take.

## Winner (113 characters)

The fire drill took four minutes to evacuate the building and forty minutes to convince anyone to go back inside.

## Runner-up (94 characters)

I replied-all to 4,000 people saying 'please remove me from this thread.' I am now the thread.

## Why the winner won

The runner-up is the tightest line in the field and its turn ("I am now the
thread") is clean, but reply-all disasters are a heavily mined genre, so it
loses on freshness. The winner sets up an efficiency story — a building
emptied in four minutes — then reverses it: the hard part was never getting
people out, it was getting them back in. That reversal is unexpected, it
rings true for anyone who has ever loitered outside after a fire drill, and
the four-minutes/forty-minutes symmetry keeps every word earning its place.
