A tiny Python program that writes one office joke to `/output/entry.txt`: a single line of at most 199 characters about office life, built as a real joke with a setup and a turn at the end. The line was picked by drafting fourteen candidates and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria — a real joke rather than an observation, a turn somewhere unexpected, a truth about the office people recognise, every word earning its place, and a fresh take rather than a stock premise. The winner and runner-up are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
